Photo requirements

Photos for a damaged item

Clear photos help the store understand what happened. Start with the affected item, then add the package, shipping label, and close-up details if you have them.

Required

Damaged item

Show the whole item and the damage clearly. Add another angle if one photo does not show both.

Outer packaging

Show the full box or bag and any crushed, torn, or wet areas.

Shipping label

A readable label can help match the package to your request. Keep payment cards and identity documents out of the frame.

Show the whole package

  • Photograph the full outer box or bag before taking close-ups.
  • Include the damaged area and keep the shipping label attached when possible.
  • If the item came in a separate bag or product box, show that packaging too.

Show the item and the damage

  • Take at least one clear photo of the damaged item.
  • Show the item with its packaging first, then add close-ups of cracks, dents, tears, or other damage.
  • Use enough light to keep the affected area in focus.

If you cannot use a camera

Choose I cannot use a camera in the request form and explain the problem in the text field. The store will review your explanation manually. You do not need to attach a photo when using that option.
