Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Shipping Protection and its order support portal. It covers this service; the store’s own privacy policy also applies to your shopping and checkout.

Who handles your information

The store where you placed your order reviews Shipping Protection requests, with support from the customer service team operating this portal and the providers that run its systems. Your order confirmation identifies the store. For questions about this service or your information, email customersupport@reply.us-seel.com and include the store name and order reference.

Information used by this service

  • Order and delivery details: your name, email address, shipping address, order reference, purchased items, protection fee, payment and refund status, and shipment and tracking information received from the store.
  • Request details: the issue you report, affected items, requested amount, descriptions, messages, and photos or other documents you submit, together with review decisions and request history.
  • Optional location: coordinates, accuracy and capture time, only when you choose to share your location in the request form and grant browser permission. You can submit a request without sharing your location.
  • Service and access records: order access links, session identifiers, communication preferences, and technical records needed to operate the service and investigate errors or misuse.

Camera permission is requested when you choose to take a photo. Photos you submit become part of your supporting material. Avoid sending full payment card numbers, passwords, identity documents or unrelated personal information.

How information is used

Information is used to identify your protected order, display shipment and request details, assess supporting material, communicate about your order or request, record refund decisions, protect access to the portal, and resolve service issues. Requests are reviewed by the store; submitting a request does not automatically approve or issue a refund.

Who can receive it

Relevant information is available to the store and authorized customer service staff handling your order or request. Hosting, storage, email and commerce platform providers process information needed to run these functions. Information may also need to be provided to authorities where required by applicable law.

If you open a carrier or third-party tracking page, that provider handles information under its own privacy policy. Its page may receive technical information from your browser and the tracking reference needed to show your shipment.

Cookies and browser storage

The shopping component uses browser storage to remember your protection choice for the cart and coordinate it with checkout. The order portal uses a session cookie to keep access to your order after you open your private email link. Blocking cookies or clearing storage may require reopening that link or choosing your protection preference again. Keep private order links confidential.

Keeping and protecting records

Order, request, evidence and communication records are kept for service administration, request review, follow-up, dispute handling and applicable recordkeeping needs. The period depends on the record and the store’s obligations; expiry of an access link does not itself delete the underlying records. Contact support to ask about retention or deletion for your order.

Access to order requests and submitted evidence is restricted through order access sessions and staff permissions. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can decline optional location sharing and manage camera access in your browser. Where available, use the unsubscribe link in service emails to change email preferences. You can also contact support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, or ask about other rights available under the law that applies to you. We may need to verify your identity and order before responding, and some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or unresolved disputes. You may contact your local data protection authority about a privacy concern.

Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date. For privacy questions, contact customersupport@reply.us-seel.com.
